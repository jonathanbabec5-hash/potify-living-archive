# The Living Archive marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Readers learning the craft and archival designers. **Offer.** Make the full Seed → New Cycle journey explorable as an illustrated archive.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Show the same plant story at Roots, Quiet Room and Cure with a link to the full cycle. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Explore three stages. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 12 readers reach three stages. |

### Sales test

Ask which three stages readers would hang as a print set. Seek three price-confirmed requests before commissioning a print run. Test demand for a twelve-stage print set using an opt-in sample, then count fulfilled print orders.

### Measurement and decision

Use `living-archive-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Display assets are a dated version. An illustration update belongs in a later reviewed revision. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
